# DESIGN RATIONALE

1. **Visual language:** parallel rules, indexed evidence, intersections, and
   explicit ordering make concurrency structural rather than decorative.
2. **Density:** continuous data bands and tables keep the workspace compact and
   scannable without imitating an overloaded monitoring wall.
3. **Color:** muted industrial amber indicates selection and causality; restrained
   green and rust-red are reserved for semantic status.
4. **Typography:** sober sans-serif carries navigation and hierarchy, while the
   system monospace carries histories, versions, contracts, IDs, and verdicts.
5. **Concurrency:** agent lanes share one ruler and expose reads from the same
   version before their effects become visible.
6. **Failure:** local `SUCCESS` remains visually separate from the global contract
   violation, reinforcing the project's central thesis.
7. **Anti-template decisions:** no gradients, ornamental blobs, nested cards,
   oversized pills, generic feature grid, or decorative dashboard metrics.
8. **Product thesis:** every primary view moves from evidence to constraints to a
   reduced explanation, presenting AgentSerial as reliability infrastructure.
