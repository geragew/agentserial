THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES

Individual success is not a system guarantee.

When agents work in parallel, they may read the same budget, reserve the same resource, act on revoked permission, or update incompatible parts of a configuration. Every task completes successfully while the combined state becomes invalid.

AgentSerial turns that action history into verifiable evidence. It checks feasible orders against a global contract, separates real violations from inconsistent histories, and reduces the result to an understandable counterexample, locally and without sending your data anywhere.
